This Privacy Policy explains the data practices of MergeVerse ("we", "us", "our") regarding MergeVerse: The Ultimate Evolution (Application ID: com.mergeverse.game).

We are committed to total transparency and data minimization. This policy accurately reflects the code, dependencies, and actual operations of the application.

3. What Information We Process & Do Not Collect

We strictly minimize data handling. You can play all puzzle game modes completely anonymously without registering an account or transmitting personal data.

  • Information We DO NOT Collect: We do NOT collect your legal name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, precise or approximate GPS location, biometric data, camera captures, microphone recordings, or Android Advertising ID (AAID).
  • Pseudonymous Local Game Profile: A random local identifier (e.g., mv-xxxx) generated strictly on your device to maintain your local achievements and game progress. You may optionally pick a display nickname and emoji avatar stored solely on your device.
  • Gameplay State: Matrix tile numbers, current scores, high scores, combo multipliers, unlocked cosmetic themes, virtual coin balance, gem balance, and power-up inventory. All gameplay state is stored locally.

4. On-Device Local Data Storage

MergeVerse uses standard on-device web and client storage APIs (specifically HTML5 localStorage and client cache) to maintain your game progress between play sessions:

  • Player progression: Current level, experience points (XP), high scores per puzzle mode.
  • Economy state: Virtual Gold Coins, Gems, Energy counter, and power-ups.
  • Audio and visual preferences: Volume sliders, mute toggles, haptic feedback setting, selected board themes.
  • Local counters: Games played count and local timer stamps.
All local game data remains exclusively on your physical device. No gameplay data is transmitted to cloud databases or remote servers.

5. Third-Party Services & SDK Audit

In accordance with Google Play developer policies, we disclose our third-party code and SDK usage:

  • Third-Party Ad Networks: None. The application does NOT bundle or initialize Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, IronSource, or any external advertising SDKs.
  • Third-Party Analytics SDKs: None. The application does NOT integrate Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook Pixel, AppsFlyer, Adjust, or similar tracking libraries.
  • Google Play Billing: NOT CONNECTED. No payment processing libraries or billing APIs are active.
  • Capacitor Runtime: Standard open-source mobile runtime plugins (SplashScreen, StatusBar, App) used solely to render the WebView and manage app lifecycle.

6. In-Game Advertisements & Rewards

MergeVerse does not serve external targeted network advertisements:

  • Any reward simulation overlays (such as voluntary "Watch Ad" to claim bonus virtual coins or extra energy) are handled locally by the game engine.
  • No user profiling, cross-app tracking, or advertising identifiers (AAID) are accessed, collected, or shared.

7. In-App Purchases & Billing Status

Status: Google Play Billing is NOT CONNECTED.

MergeVerse does NOT offer real-money in-app purchases or subscriptions. All items, cosmetic themes, and boosters in the game's shop are acquired entirely with virtual currencies (Gold Coins, Gems) earned by playing the game. No credit card, debit card, or financial account details are ever requested, collected, or processed.

8. Analytics & Behavioral Tracking

We do not engage in behavioral tracking or telemetry collection:

  • No third-party analytics libraries are embedded in the app.
  • Gameplay metrics (such as high score and total merges) are displayed solely on your device for your own satisfaction.
  • Standard, aggregated, non-personally identifiable crash reports may be provided by the Google Play Console (Android Vitals) if you have opted in at the Android system level.

9. Cookies & Local Storage

MergeVerse does not use advertising or tracking cookies. When accessed on web or within the Android WebView container, the app utilizes standard HTML5 localStorage strictly for functional purposes (saving your puzzle boards, score records, and audio settings).

10. How Information Is Used

Any local data processed on your device is used solely to:

  • Run and display puzzle game modes (2048 Classic, Number Snacks, Hexagon, Merge Blocks).
  • Save your puzzle grid, high scores, unlocked themes, and virtual coin balance.
  • Remember your sound, music, and haptic feedback settings.

11. Data Sharing & Disclosure

Because all gameplay data remains locally on your device, we do not sell, rent, trade, or transmit your personal data to any third party.

12. Data Security

Your game save files are isolated within the secure, sandboxed storage directory assigned to MergeVerse by your device's operating system. No sensitive personal or financial credentials are held by the application.

13. Data Retention

Because MergeVerse is offline-first, your game data is stored directly on your personal device and persists until you choose to reset it or uninstall the application. We maintain no remote servers storing your gameplay data.

14. Data Deletion & Reset Instructions

You have complete control over your saved data. You may delete all locally stored game progress, high scores, and settings at any time using any of the following methods:

  • In-App Reset: Open the game's Profile / Settings menu and select Reset Local Data to immediately clear all local progression.
  • Android System Settings: Open your device's Settings → Apps → MergeVerse → Storage → Clear Data / Clear Storage.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling MergeVerse from your device permanently removes the application along with all locally stored game files.

If you have any questions regarding data deletion, please contact the developer at Nexusoft.company@gmail.com.

15. Children's Privacy & Family Safety

MergeVerse is a mathematical puzzle game designed for casual audiences of all ages:

  • We do not collect personal identifiable information from any user, including children.
  • There is no open public chat, multiplayer messaging, or user-generated content sharing.
  • No third-party behavioral advertising or tracking is used.
  • No violent, scary, or mature content is present in any game mode.

16. Permissions & Device Hardware Access

The Android application requests only the minimum permissions required for core gameplay:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide tactile haptic feedback during tile merges, wheel spins, and combo triggers. You can toggle haptics on or off in game settings.
  • The application does NOT request camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or location permissions.

17. Offline-First Architecture

MergeVerse is engineered so that gameplay does not require an active internet connection:

  • All game modes launch and function seamlessly in Airplane Mode or without mobile data.
  • Your merges, level-ups, and achievements are saved locally without interruption.

18. User Choices & Controls

You have full autonomy over your experience:

  • Audio & Haptic Controls: Sound effects, background music, and haptic vibration can be toggled at any time from the home screen and settings menu.
  • Data Reset: You can wipe all local progression at any time via the in-app settings or your device application settings.
  • Offline Play: You can play indefinitely without an internet connection or account sign-in.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new puzzle modes or changes in legal regulations. When changes occur, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will be updated, and notice will be provided in the in-app Policy Center.
